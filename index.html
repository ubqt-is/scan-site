Process

Four steps from site visit to delivered file.

Scope

You send the address or object, the scope, rough size, and deadline. We recommend a capture method and quote a fixed price.

Capture

On-site visit with the right kit for the scale. Most interiors are captured in a single visit; objects can be shipped or scanned on site.

Process

Registration, cleanup, and conversion. You get a preview link to check coverage before final delivery.

Deliver

Files in the formats you named, with units and coordinate notes. Typical turnaround is one to five business days depending on scale.

About

A capture practice inside an XR engineering studio.

Digital Ubiquities is a Long Beach–based creative technology studio best known for VR clinical simulators deployed at NYU and other universities. Scanning grew out of that work: simulation scenes need real geometry, and the studio has been capturing it — daily, for years — long before it was a service.

The practice is led by an engineer with dual degrees in Physics and Mechanical Engineering and twelve years across XR development, interactive installations, and fabrication. Studio work has been juried and awarded at SIGGRAPH, AWE, and MIT Reality Hack. See the simulation side at xr.ubqt.is.
